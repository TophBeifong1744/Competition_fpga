# sat_add contract
One rising-edge clock; synchronous active-high reset has priority over all handshakes and clears out_valid and y. Reset discards a pending transaction.
Inputs a and b are signed 8-bit integers. Accepted payload result is mathematical integer a+b clipped to [-128,127]. No rounding. All 65536 defined operand pairs are supported.
Outside reset, input acceptance is in_valid && in_ready; output acceptance is out_valid && out_ready. One registered slot: in_ready = !out_valid || out_ready, including during reset (reset still suppresses acceptance). Empty accepts even when downstream not ready. Full can consume and replace in the same edge. Full stalled retains valid and y. Output becomes visible after accepting edge and earliest output handshake is the next rising edge (one cycle); initiation interval one cycle without stalls.
TB drives falling edges, checks old output before scoreboard removal/insertion at rising edges, checks new state 1 ns after NBA. Independent golden uses signed integers and comparisons to decimal limits. Producer preserves its offered payload until accepted.
Control inputs must be 0/1. Accepted operands must be known 0/1; X/Z accepted payload is unsupported and TB fails rather than coercing. Invalid or reset-discarded a/b may be X/Z and must not contaminate observable state. All observed valid/ready and valid payload use case inequality and explicit unknown guards. Separate disposable X/Z output mutants test checker sensitivity.
Acceptance: initial and in-flight reset, idle, exhaustive operand pairs, explicit clipping transitions, 32-cycle stall, simultaneous replace, fixed-seed traffic, drain, accounting accepted=consumed+reset_discarded, exact cycle state and full-rate checks. Watchdog and nonzero checks required. Invalid y is unconstrained except reset and hold states established by implementation-independent valid-output checking.
Synthesis, timing, CDC, hardware and system integration are outside this behavioral simulation.
